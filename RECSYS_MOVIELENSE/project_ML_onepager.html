Project 2 — Movie Recommendation with Your Transformer

Reuse the transformer you built in Project 1.  •  Works on CPU.  •  Estimated time: 10–12 hours.

Treat each movie as a "token" and a user's watch history as a "sentence": next-token prediction becomes next-movie prediction. You train your Project-1 transformer on MovieLens, pull out the learned item embeddings, build a tool that returns movies similar to a seed movie, study that space with unsupervised methods, and compare against classic baselines.

What you'll build (in tinytransformer/rec/)

  • A · Data → tensors — turn histories into right-padded next-item training pairs; sample batches.
  • B · Training — the next-item training loop (padded targets ignored in the loss).
  • C · Embeddings — extract the learned item vectors (you'll compare the input vs. output embedding).
  • D · Similarity tool — top-k cosine neighbours of a seed movie.
  • E · Unsupervised structure — k-means over the embeddings + a genre-purity score.
  • F · Baselines & evaluation — most-popular and item-item kNN, compared on hit@10 / nDCG@10 / MRR.

The data download, ranking metrics, and t-SNE plotting are provided. Look for ### BEGIN CODE EDIT ### markers.
